============================================================
           KINGDOMCORE COMMANDS & PERMISSIONS
============================================================

------------------------------------------------------------
 [ PLAYER COMMANDS ] - Available to all by default
------------------------------------------------------------

/shop [type] (Aliases: /s)
  - Description: Opens the main shop menu or a specific shop.
  - Subcommands:
      • /shop wood      - Buy wood, leaves, and saplings.
      • /shop stone     - Buy stones, bricks, and ores.
      • /shop fisherman - Buy fish, rods, and aquatic items.
      • /shop fletcher  - Buy bows, arrows, and fletching gear.
      • /shop redstone  - Buy redstone components and machinery.
      • /shop farming   - Buy seeds, food, and farming tools.
      • /shop armor     - Buy armor sets and defense gear.
      • /shop enchant   - Buy enchanted items and magic gear.
      • /shop potion    - Buy potions and brewing ingredients.
      • /shop nether    - Buy nether-specific blocks and drops.
      • /shop end       - Buy end-specific materials.
      • /shop sell      - Open the Bulk Sell GUI to earn Shards.
      • /shop convert   - Open the Gem/Shard Converter GUI.

/ah [sell <price>] (Aliases: /auctionhouse)
  - Description: Opens the Auction House or puts an item up for sale.
  - Permission: kingdomcore.ah

/bounty <player> <amount>
  - Description: Places a bounty on a player using Shards.
  - Permission: kingdomcore.bounty

/sb (Aliases: /scoreboard)
  - Description: Toggles the RPG Scoreboard visibility.
  - Permission: kingdomcore.sb

/verify <code>
  - Description: Links your Minecraft account to the webstore.
  - Permission: kingdomcore.verify

/resourcepack (Aliases: /rp, /resource)
  - Description: Prompts the download of the server resource pack.
  - Permission: None (Available to everyone)

/craft (Aliases: /forge, /soulforge)
  - Description: Opens the Soul Forge crafting interface.
  - Permission: kingdomcore.craft

/eco (Aliases: /balance, /bal)
  - Description: Check your current Shards and Gems.
  - Permission: None (Available to everyone)

/eco pay <player> <amount>
  - Description: Transfer Shards to another player.
  - Permission: None (Available to everyone)

/eco paygems <player> <amount>
  - Description: Transfer Gems to another player.
  - Permission: None (Available to everyone)

/eco note <amount>
  - Description: Withdraw Shards into a physical item (Money Note).
  - Permission: None (Available to everyone)


------------------------------------------------------------
 [ ADMIN COMMANDS ] - Requires OP or specific permission
------------------------------------------------------------

/admin (Aliases: None)
  - Description: Opens the main Admin Panel GUI.
  - Permission: kingdomcore.admin

/admin player <name>
  - Description: Directly manage a specific player's data.
  - Permission: kingdomcore.admin

/shop reload
  - Description: Reloads shop configurations from database/resources.
  - Permission: kingdomcore.admin

/shop migrate
  - Description: Migrates local YAML shop files into MongoDB.
  - Permission: kingdomcore.admin

/eco give <player> <shards|gems|level> <amount>
  - Description: Grant currency or levels to a player.
  - Permission: kingdomcore.admin

/eco take <player> <shards|gems|level> <amount>
  - Description: Subtract currency or levels from a player.
  - Permission: kingdomcore.admin

/eco set <player> <shards|gems|level> <amount>
  - Description: Set a player's exact currency or level.
  - Permission: kingdomcore.admin

/eco wipe <shards|gems|levels>
  - Description: Wipe specific data server-wide (Use with caution!).
  - Permission: kingdomcore.admin

/eco spawnshard <amount>
  - Description: Spawn a physical Shard item at your location.
  - Permission: kingdomcore.admin

/eco airdrop <tier1|tier2|tier3|tier4>
  - Description: Trigger a global economy airdrop event.
  - Permission: kingdomcore.admin

/reset confirm
  - Description: Permanently resets ALL player data in the database.
  - Permission: kingdomcore.admin (Requires OP)
